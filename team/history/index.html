Team History

A tradition of Coyettes pride.

The Anna Coyettes have proudly carried their team spirit since the 1950s.

That legacy lives on through generations of dancers, families, and supporters who celebrate the Coyettes. As more stories and historical details are gathered, this page can grow to recognize the people and moments that shaped the team.
