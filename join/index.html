Join the Coyettes

Ready to dance with the Coyettes?

Start building your skills, learn about the team pathways, and get in touch for the latest tryout information.

Your Coyettes journey starts with a love of dance, a willingness to learn, and a team-first spirit.

Students and families can use this guide to explore the program and begin preparing. Team placement, eligibility, and tryout expectations are set by the school and may change each year, so contact the Coyettes Director for the latest official details.

Program Pathways

Two ways to grow as a dancer.

Tryout skills differ by team. Use these lists as a guide while you build your technique.

Varsity

Coyettes Varsity Drill Team

  • Right and left: splits, leaps, pirouettes, battements, high kicks, fan kicks, and press leap.
  • Technique: basic ballet terminology.
  • Right side: Russian, calypso, chaîné jeté, and tour.
JV

JV Team

  • Right and left: single pirouette, battements, and chaîné turns.
  • Right side: split, leap, and press leap.
Start Preparing

Build a strong foundation.

Consistent instruction and safe practice can help you feel more prepared.

Grades 5–8

Explore dance opportunities

Look into ADT and take classes at a local dance studio to build technique and confidence.

Grades 9–11

Plan for next year

Ask about including dance in your school schedule and consider classes at a local dance studio.

Working on flexibility? Stretch gradually with guidance from a qualified dance instructor; never force a split or stretch through pain.
